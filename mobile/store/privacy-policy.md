# Privacy Policy: Patang Rush

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Patang Rush ("the app") is a game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Information we collect

**None.** The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It has no sign-in, no ads and no analytics.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves two small things **only on your phone**:

- your best score on each difficulty level
- whether sound is on or off

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: short buzzes when you grab a star or crash. You can turn vibration off in your phone's settings.
- **Internet**: included by the app framework. The game does not send or receive any data.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The app does not share any information with third parties, because it collects none.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting information (for example, online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date above will change.
