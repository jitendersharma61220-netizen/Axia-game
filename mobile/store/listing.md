## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Fly your patang over the city!

Patang Rush is a one-tap kite game set in Gurugram. Tap anywhere for a gust of wind and your kite rises. Let go and it sinks. Slip through the gaps between the towers. Touch a tower or the street and the run is over.

🪁 ONE TAP, EASY TO START
The first towers are gentle, so anyone can play in seconds. Then the city speeds up, the gaps shrink, and it gets tricky.

📍 FLY ACROSS GURUGRAM
Every stage is a new stop: MG Road, IFFCO Chowk, Sector 48, Cyber City, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road and more. Green road signs show where you are, in Hindi and English.

⭐ GRAB STARS FOR BONUS POINTS
Stars float between the towers. Take a risk and grab them.

🏙️ NEW CHALLENGES AS YOU GO
Sliding gaps from stage 4. Rival kites from stage 6. How far can you fly?

🎯 THREE DIFFICULTY LEVELS
Normal, Hard and Insane. Your best score on each is saved.

✈️ PLAYS OFFLINE
No internet needed. No sign-up. No ads.

Perfect for a quick game in the metro, between meetings, or with friends: who can fly the farthest?
